5. For predictable audience reach, use Meta Ads Manager after the organic post flow is verified. A useful campaign type for a dealership is click-to-WhatsApp or lead generation aimed only at countries/cities you can actually ship to.
6. Never buy fake followers or fake engagement.
7. Do not run paid ads until the landing vehicle page, quote flow, WhatsApp reply path and tracking/CRM are confirmed.

Suggested organic content mix:
- New arrival with 5+ real photos
- Short walk-around video if available
- Price + key specs
- Shipping destination examples only when the rate is verified/clearly estimated
- Sold/delivered proof from real transactions (with customer consent)
- Educational posts: how China-to-Africa shipping works, documents, port choices

The website cannot guarantee Facebook reach. Reach is controlled by Meta and audience demand; paid distribution requires explicit ad setup/budget.
